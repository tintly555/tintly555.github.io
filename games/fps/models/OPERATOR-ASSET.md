# Shared player character

- Model: [Male Soldier 01](https://models.codelove.in/models/military-male-01/), a rigged male military character by CodeLove, distributed under MIT. The GLB identifies its source as Microsoft, copyright 2020, and embeds the MIT license.
- Animation clips: matching Biped-rig idle, walk, and run GLBs from the model's [complete pack](https://models.codelove.in/models/military-male-01/), also covered by the pack's MIT license.
- The model keeps its original uniform, helmet, textures, and real skinned hands, with a subtle width adjustment for a sturdier silhouette. Third-person weapons are bound to its Biped hands. First-person views use the game's fitted gloves and sleeves attached to the weapons, with corrected camera-space arm targets so the hands stay visible. The bundled clips drive the shared third-person character.
